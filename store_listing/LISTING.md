## Full description — English
Chakri AI is an AI-powered career assistant for job seekers in Bangladesh — government or private, everything you need to prepare in one app.

✅ **Resume Maker** – enter your details and AI drafts a professional resume; save or share it as PDF.
✅ **Cover Letter Generator** – tailored letters in Bangla or English for a specific role and company.
✅ **Mock Interview Coach** – practise with an AI interviewer and get feedback on each answer plus a final summary.
✅ **Job Matching & Skill Gap** – job suggestions based on your skills and an analysis of what to learn next.
✅ **Job Circulars** – search and filter circulars and see application deadlines.
✅ **Question Bank & Study Material** – subject-wise exam questions with answers and explanations.

⚠️ **Important:** AI-generated content can be wrong — please verify it. Circular information is collected from official sources (e.g. bpsc.gov.bd); always check the original notice before applying. This app does not represent any government body or employer.

Bangla-first design. Contact: shahriar.sharif406@gmail.com
